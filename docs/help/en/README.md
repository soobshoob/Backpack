# Backpack Help

Backpack is a toolkit for clear presentations, tutorial videos and screen demos: it shows the keys and mouse buttons you press, highlights an area with Focus, magnifies around the pointer, lets you draw over the screen with a smart pen, shapes and numbered steps, and extends with plugins. Backpack was called KeyOverlay before; your settings, profiles and styles are moved automatically. This help describes only what already works.

## Using the help

- Press F1 in a Backpack window (main window, diagnostics, the help itself), click “Help” at the bottom of the menu or choose “Help” in the tray icon menu. In the main window, F1 opens the topic for the current page.
- Pressing F1 again does not open a second window: the open help comes to the front and goes to the right topic.
- Search and contents are on the left, the text is on the right. Search (Ctrl+F) looks through all topics and picture captions and ignores case.
- “Back” and “Forward” (Alt+← and Alt+→) follow your history. “A−” and “A+” change the text size.
- Click a picture to enlarge it; there you also get “Actual size” and Ctrl + mouse wheel zoom.
- “Show example” links open a separate window with an example on made-up keys. Your typing is not used and settings are not changed.
- External links open in your browser only after you confirm. The help works offline.
- F1 is not captured globally: when another program is active, F1 stays with it.

The help language follows the interface language (the “App settings” page). Pictures are drawings of Backpack’s own windows and elements with example data; they contain none of your windows or desktop. Pictures follow the light or dark theme of the windows.

The same texts are in the repository under `docs/help/` and can be read on GitHub.
